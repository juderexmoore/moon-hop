# Moon Hop

A family-built browser game: climb from the bottom of the sea through the jungle, mountains,
sky and space, past the Moon, Mars and the Sun, and beat the Black Hole.
Every round, a family member added one new idea.

Works on computers, iPads and iPhones (on-screen buttons appear on touch screens).

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages link for this repo, or open `index.html` in a browser.

## Controls

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Arrows or A / D | Move |
| Space, Up or W | Jump (hold to jump higher) |
| F or J | Shoot |
| Ctrl + B | Cast the dog's spell |
| V | Switch dog spell |
| P or Esc | Pause |
| S (title screen) | Sandbox: build your own levels and characters |

**Mouse:** the climber runs toward the mouse. Left click jumps (hold to jump higher), right click shoots,
middle click casts the dog's spell, and the wheel switches spells. Menus have buttons you can click.

**Arcade mode (A on the title screen):** one life, no checkpoints, and it never ends. Past the Black Hole you
climb on through Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, the Kuiper Belt and a nebula into the Endless Void. It has its own top 10.

**Damage %:** every hit adds to your damage meter (bottom right). The higher it is, the farther hits knock you,
so watch out at 100%+. Every 100 m heals 30%.

**Explore mode (X on the title screen):** walk around a 3D world (arrows or WASD, F shoots, Space talks, M map).
Do quests for the people with a **!** to earn coins, then spend them at Bolt's Shop on gear for exploring
or on power-ups for your next climb. Coins and gear save in your browser.

**Portals:** now and then a portal appears on a platform. Jump in for one of 10 mini-games. Win one and you
keep a power-up until you fall, and they stack.

High scores, levels and characters save in your own browser.

## Playing at home with saved files

Double-click `Play Moon Hop.bat` (Windows). It runs the game at http://localhost:8765/
and saves the leaderboard, levels and characters to `scores.json` and `sandbox.json` in this folder.
